This policy explains what information Hueborn: Loot Legends ("the game", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. We collect only what the game needs to save your progress, run leaderboards and online features, and keep players safe. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How we use the information

  • To save and restore your game progress across devices.
  • To show leaderboards and your rank.
  • To run online features such as multiplayer rooms (playing together on maps and in the Trade Plaza), chat, friends and trading between players.
  • To deliver items you purchase.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: detecting cheating, investigating reports, and moderating chat.
  • To fix problems and answer your support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Who processes your information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that run parts of the game for us, under their own privacy terms:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: player accounts, cloud save, leaderboards, multiplayer rooms through Lobby and Relay, and chat). Unity privacy policy
  • Google (Google Play, Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing). Google privacy policy

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect players and the game from fraud or abuse. Your display name, scores and chat messages are visible to other players by design.

4. Chat and player interaction

The game lets players see each other, play together, talk to each other and trade items. Messages you send are delivered to other players and may be stored for a limited time so that we can review reports of harassment, scams or other abuse. Please do not share personal information (real name, address, phone number, passwords) in chat. If another player harasses or scams you, report them in the game or by email (see Contact). We may mute or ban accounts that break the rules.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, save data and scores: while your account exists. Accounts with no activity for 24 months may be deleted.
  • Chat messages: up to 30 days, unless they are part of an open abuse report.
  • Purchase records: as long as required for accounting and legal purposes.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Deleting your account and data

In the game

Open Settings → DELETE MY DATA and confirm. This permanently deletes your online account, your cloud save and your leaderboard entries, and resets the progress stored on your device.

Without the game

Email cxstudiodev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Hueborn data" and include your hero name and, if you can, the Player ID shown in Settings. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email. Purchase records we are legally required to keep are retained only as long as the law requires.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it using the same address. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may have further rights, including the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Hueborn: Loot Legends is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the game.
